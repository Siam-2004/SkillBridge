Help centre

Money

How do I add SkillCoin?

Send the amount to the bKash or Nagad number shown on the deposit page, then submit the amount, the number you sent from, and the provider's transaction ID. An administrator confirms the transfer arrived and your balance is credited. Each transaction ID can be claimed once.

Why is some of my balance "reserved"?

Because it is committed. Publishing a job reserves its budget; requesting a withdrawal reserves the amount while the payout is prepared. It is still your money — it just cannot be spent twice.

How do I withdraw?

Request a payout to your bKash or Nagad number. The amount is reserved immediately, and an administrator sends the money and records the provider's transaction ID. If a payout fails, the reservation returns to your available balance.

Work

Can I change an agreement after accepting it?

Not unilaterally. Raise a modification or a deadline extension; it takes effect when the other party approves it, and the previous terms are kept.

What happens if I miss the 48-hour window?

The payment for that task releases to the freelancer automatically. Request a revision or raise a dispute before the window closes if you are not happy.

Who can see my project files?

Only the client, the hired freelancer and active team members. Files are served through a permission check every single time — there is no public URL for a private file.

Disputes

What does raising a dispute do?

It freezes the escrow on that project immediately, so nothing can be released while it is open. Both sides give a statement and attach evidence, and an administrator decides: release, refund, split, or continue to hold.

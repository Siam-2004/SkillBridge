Terms of service

These terms describe how Skillbridge works. They are written plainly on purpose.

1. Accounts

You register as either a client or a freelancer, and you verify your email address before you can post a job, send a proposal, or move SkillCoin. You are responsible for what happens under your account.

2. SkillCoin

SkillCoin is an internal accounting unit: one SkillCoin equals one Bangladeshi taka. It is not a security, not a cryptocurrency, and carries no interest. It enters the platform when a deposit is verified and leaves when a withdrawal is paid.

3. Job budgets

Publishing a job reserves its full budget. Cancelling before anybody is hired returns the reservation in full. After a freelancer is hired, neither party may cancel unilaterally.

4. Agreements

An accepted agreement is binding on both parties and cannot be edited in place. Changes require a modification request approved by the other party, and every version is retained.

5. Escrow and payment

Funding a project moves the agreed amount into escrow, where neither party controls it. Task payments are drawn from escrow only. A client is never charged twice for work that escrow already covers.

6. The review window

A client has 48 hours from submission to approve, request a revision, or raise a dispute. If none of those happens, the payment is released automatically as agreed at the outset.

7. Disputes

Either party may raise a dispute, which freezes the relevant escrow. A Skillbridge administrator reviews the agreement, the messages, the submissions and the payment history, and issues a decision with a written reason.

8. Records

Financial records, agreements, submissions and completed-project conversations are retained as evidence and are not deleted.

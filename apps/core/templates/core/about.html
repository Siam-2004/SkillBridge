Our Mission

A marketplace where money is never a question.

SkillBridge is built on one simple principle: work starts only when funding is already locked in escrow, and payment is released only on terms both sides explicitly agreed to.

🔒

1. Budget Guaranteed

Clients fund the job before it publishes. Freelancers never spend hours writing proposals or working on projects where the money doesn't exist.

📝

2. Locked Agreements

Scope, price, milestones, and revisions are locked in written, versioned agreements. Changing terms requires mutual consent from both parties.

⚡

3. 48-Hour Auto Release

When work is delivered, clients have 48 hours to approve or request a revision. Silence is not leverage — payments auto-release when terms are fulfilled.

What is SkillCoin?

One SkillCoin equals exactly one Bangladeshi Taka (1 SkillCoin = 1 BDT). It is a transparent internal platform ledger unit, not a cryptocurrency and not an investment asset.

SkillCoin enters the platform when a deposit is verified and exits when a withdrawal is paid out. Every single movement is recorded on an immutable double-entry ledger that both parties can audit.

The 48-Hour Escrow Protection Rule

When a freelancer submits completed work, the client has 48 hours to inspect it. They can approve it (releasing payment instantly), request a revision (pausing the timer), or open a dispute. If the client does nothing, the platform auto-releases the milestone payment to the freelancer. Silence is never used as leverage on SkillBridge.

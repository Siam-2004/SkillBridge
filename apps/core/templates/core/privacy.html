Privacy policy

What we hold

  • Your account details: name, email address, username, and role.
  • Your profile: what you have chosen to publish about your work.
  • Your activity: jobs, proposals, agreements, messages, tasks and files.
  • Your money: deposits, withdrawals, and every SkillCoin movement.

Who can see it

Your public profile, portfolio and reviews are visible to anyone. Everything else is visible only to the people involved: messages to their participants, project files to project members, and your wallet to you.

The assistant

The built-in assistant has no direct access to the database. It answers from a context document assembled for your specific question and checked against your permissions first. It is never given private messages, wallet balances, another client's data, or authentication details.

Administrators

Administrators can see what they need to verify payments, moderate content and resolve disputes. Sensitive administrative actions are recorded in an append-only audit trail.

Retention

Financial records, agreements and completed-project evidence are kept, so a dispute can be settled on what actually happened. Requesting deletion of an account does not erase the financial record it is part of.
